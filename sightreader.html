Ready for your rehearsal routine

Start with your own part.

Start with your own part: read original pages, adjust structured notation, and add or edit rehearsal marks with SightReader Pro.

01

Read the original clearly.

The free edition includes PDF and uncompressed MusicXML import, plus scanning paper scores to image PDFs. PDFs preserve the source page, with zoom, pan and manual turns. MusicXML offers note-size and density controls, with lines and pages fitted to the reading viewport.

02

Add and edit rehearsal marks with SightReader Pro.

SightReader Pro lets you add and edit PencilKit annotations; source scores and drawings stay on this iPad. Free users can view existing annotations read-only, but cannot add or edit them. Scanning saves paper scores as image PDFs; recognition into reflowable notation is not provided.

03

Choose scores with visible sources.

SightReader Pro includes local search of the bundled OpenScore / Mutopia catalog snapshot. Review parts, licenses and attribution before choosing a download. The current catalog focuses on violin PDFs. Free users can still import or scan their own scores; catalog search is a Pro feature.

The 1.0 plan

Core reading. Professional playing.

Core reading is free. SightReader Pro is planned as a one-time in-app purchase, with US$4.99 as the worldwide reference price; local-currency prices will be shown in the App Store at release.

Free core edition

PDF and uncompressed MusicXML import, scanning to image PDFs, reading, read-only access to existing annotations, basic score management, reading progress and backup. The free edition supports up to 10 user scores; built-in examples do not count. Existing scores above the limit will not be deleted.

SightReader Pro

Add and edit PencilKit annotations, and search the bundled score catalog. SightReader Pro has no score-count limit. Automatic page turning and a two-page spread across two iPads are planned. There is no subscription; restore the purchase on a second iPad with the same Apple ID without paying again.

SightReader Pro is still in preparation and is not available to purchase. Automatic following is limited to supported monophonic MusicXML; PDFs and scans use manual turns. Automatic following and two-iPad features have not completed physical-device acceptance. Final compatibility will be confirmed before release.

Which files can I import?

The current build accepts PDF and .xml / .musicxml files smaller than 50,000,000 bytes. Compressed .mxl is not supported. Encrypted, empty or unparseable files are rejected.

Can I read after denying camera or microphone access?

Yes. Local reading and manual page turns remain available. The camera supports user-started gaze following or scanning; the microphone supports user-started performance following. Manage access in iPad settings.

What should I know about backups and deletion?

Empty Trash removes a score, its annotations and reading progress from SightReader’s local library. When iCloud sync is enabled, deletion status is sent to your devices, though sync may be delayed. Separate copies in an Apple device backup, Files, another iPad or email may remain. See section 8 of the Privacy Policy.

Can I download the app now?

SightReader is not yet released. This is a product preview; an App Store download link is not available yet.

1. Operator, contact and effective date

SightReader is provided by Zicong DAI. Effective date: October 30, 2026.

For privacy questions or requests, email ericdai2013@gmail.com. Support messages and attachments are used only to handle your request; see section 9 for retention and deletion details.

2. Scores and information stored on iPad

When you import a PDF or uncompressed MusicXML file, SightReader stores the score in the app’s local library with a title, local identifier, source details and other information used to find, display and resume it. The app also stores label assignments, annotations, reading progress, layout choices and scan adjustments needed for your library. Core reading does not require a SightReader account.

Scanning creates an image-based PDF from pages you confirm. On-device Vision text recognition may read text on the first page to suggest a score title. It does not recognize musical notes, convert a scan into structured notation or make a scanned PDF eligible for automatic following.

3. Camera, gaze and microphone

The camera is used when you start the system document scanner or live gaze following. During following, camera frames and face/gaze estimates are processed on the iPad to estimate reading position. Calibration samples remain in runtime memory and are cleared when following stops. The microphone is used only after you start live following; short audio input is analyzed on the iPad into pitch, confidence and timing.

SightReader does not record or send eye-tracking video, raw microphone audio or calibration samples to the developer. Following sensors stop when you stop following, leave the reader or send the app to the background. If you deny camera or microphone access, local reading and manual page turns remain available; scanning or the related following feature will not work.

4. Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings and are signed in to iCloud, SightReader uses Apple CloudKit’s private database to sync user-owned score files, annotations, layout and reading information, and labels assigned to each score. Bundled reference scores are not uploaded as user scores. The separate label directory is stored locally; unassigned label definitions do not sync on their own.

Your iCloud service provider and applicable terms depend on the country or region associated with your Apple Account. In mainland China, iCloud is operated by GCBD (AIPO Cloud (Guizhou) Technology Co., Ltd.); use is subject to the applicable iCloud terms and privacy policy. Apple describes CloudKit’s private database as tied to your iCloud account and not shared with others by default. Third-party apps may store and process data in iCloud under the applicable service terms and those apps’ privacy policies. iCloud data counts against your account’s storage quota. SightReader does not operate a separate score-storage server.

Turning the iCloud switch off stops this app’s future sync but does not delete data already uploaded to iCloud. Sync can be delayed or fail. When devices have conflicting edits, the app may preserve separate score copies instead of silently replacing one. Review the copies on your devices before removing data.

5. Two-iPad stand

If you choose to create or join a two-iPad stand, SightReader discovers nearby app instances and exchanges session data with the iPad you select. This can include a temporary display name and session identifier, a short pairing code and key material, a score fingerprint and format/size, page and layout details, and page-turn coordination messages. Each iPad must already have its own copy of the same score.

The stand does not send score files, titles, your library, annotations, raw audio or video, pitch data or gaze samples between iPads. The connection is encrypted and uses temporary pairing information. Stop or pause the session to disconnect and clear temporary session state.

6. Score catalog, downloads and external links

The score catalog is bundled with the app, and catalog search is performed locally. The current catalog offers violin parts; you can import PDF or MusicXML files for other instruments. When you choose a catalog PDF that is not bundled, the app requests it directly from the listed source, including GitHub’s raw file service or Mutopia’s website.

A download request necessarily reaches that source and can expose your IP address, requested file path and ordinary network request information. The app does not include your own scores, annotations or sensor input in the catalog download request. Source websites control their own server logs and retention; those details have not been confirmed for every catalog host.

Opening a source or license link takes you to the destination site in a browser. The destination may receive ordinary browser request information and apply its own privacy and cookie rules. Music and score licenses govern content use, not network logging.

7. Pro purchase and StoreKit

SightReader Pro is planned as a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase with a US$4.99 U.S. storefront price. Other storefront currencies, taxes and prices are shown by the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to request product details and localized pricing, process a purchase you initiate, and read or restore the transaction entitlement needed to unlock Pro.

Apple processes the App Store payment and associated Apple Account activity. SightReader does not ask you to enter card details in the app and does not use a separate purchase server or SightReader account. Apple handles transaction records under its own policies.

8. Trash, deletion, retention and backups

Deleting a score moves it to Trash. Its local original, annotations and reading progress remain available for restoration until you restore it or confirm Empty Trash. If iCloud sync is on, the score and its Trash state may also be present in the private iCloud database while it remains recoverable.

Empty Trash permanently removes the score and related annotations and reading progress from the app’s local library. The app retains a small local deletion record with the score identifier and sync information so an old copy is not brought back by a later sync. When iCloud sync is enabled, deletion status is sent to your devices and a deletion marker may remain with synchronization details.

A deletion marker records your choice and helps prevent an old score copy from being synced back. It does not guarantee removal of separate copies you may have on another device, in an Apple device backup, in Files, or in email, or immediate deletion of records retained by a service provider. Turning iCloud off does not remove data previously uploaded.

SightReader does not currently provide a manual score-backup or export screen. If you enable an Apple device backup, local app data may be included under Apple’s backup process and your system settings. A device backup, original file in Files, other iPad, screenshot or email is a separate copy; removing the app or emptying Trash does not necessarily remove those copies.

9. Support email

If you email support at ericdai2013@gmail.com, Google Gmail processes the message for delivery. The developer uses your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send only to handle your support or privacy request. The developer deletes these messages and attachments from the support mailbox 90 days after the case is closed, except when keeping them is necessary to handle a dispute or meet legal obligations. Gmail’s Trash and provider backups are handled under the provider’s rules.

You do not need to send your whole library. Remove unpublished scores, personal details and unrelated attachments unless they are necessary to explain the issue. You may write to ericdai2013@gmail.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of support information the developer actually holds.

10. Visiting the WERD website

The SightReader product page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends requests to GitHub to load its pages and images. The page contains no forms, advertising or analytics scripts; your appearance preference is stored in your browser’s local storage, and the selected language is reflected in the page URL.

GitHub handles website requests under its Privacy Statement. Opening an email link does not send a message until you choose to send it.

11. Your choices, requests and policy changes

You can manage local scores through the library and Trash, control optional sync with the iCloud switch, and manage camera or microphone permissions in iPad Settings. You can contact the address above about access, correction or deletion of information the developer actually holds. A request to the developer cannot erase data held by Apple, a catalog website, an email provider, a device backup or another person.
