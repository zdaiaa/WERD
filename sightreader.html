Ready for your rehearsal routine

Start with your own part.

Start with your own part: read original pages, adjust structured notation, and keep the marks you need for the next rehearsal.

01

Read the original clearly.

Import PDF or uncompressed MusicXML. PDFs preserve the source page, with zoom, pan and manual turns. MusicXML offers note-size and density controls, with lines and pages fitted to the reading viewport.

02

Keep rehearsal marks on the page.

Annotate with PencilKit, keeping source scores and drawings on this iPad. Scanning saves paper scores as image PDFs; recognition into reflowable notation is not provided.

03

Choose scores with visible sources.

Search the bundled OpenScore / Mutopia catalog snapshot locally, review parts, licenses and attribution, then choose a download. The current catalog focuses on violin PDFs; downloaded scores can be read offline.

Which files can I import?

The current build accepts PDF and .xml / .musicxml files smaller than 50,000,000 bytes. Compressed .mxl is not supported. Encrypted, empty or unparseable files are rejected.

Can I read after denying camera or microphone access?

Yes. Local reading and manual page turns remain available. The camera supports user-started gaze following or scanning; the microphone supports user-started performance following. Manage access in iPad settings.

What should I know about backups and deletion?

Backup files are unencrypted. Keep your originals and choose a trusted destination. Recently Deleted retains original files; there is currently no permanent-delete control or automatic expiry.
